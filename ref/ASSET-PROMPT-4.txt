ЗАЧЕМ
  Сейчас шапка — три отдельных блока: панель статов, столбец валют и карта.
  Между ними щели, а четыре баланса не влезают в один ряд и стоят сеткой
  2×2. Ряд при этом полупустой: полосы HP и XP короткие, а справа от них
  воздух.

  Нужна ОДНА широкая панель, которая занимает всю строку до карты и держит
  внутри всё сразу: портрет, имя, длинные полосы HP и XP и четыре валюты
  в ОДИН ряд. Карта остаётся отдельной (G1) — но пусть будет и вариант,
  где она встроена в ту же панель (G2), выберем по виду.

  Внутри — непрозрачная заливка: сквозь шапку не должно быть видно мира.

  2. свежий скриншот шапки    — три блока, щели между ними и валюты 2×2
  Первая — стиль. Вторая — что заменяем и в какой пропорции.

===PROMPT===

Use the attached game HUD mockup (the ornate dark-fantasy one) as the single
style reference. The second screenshot shows the game as it is now — it is
there to show what is being replaced and in what proportion.

Produce two separate assets, G1 and G2.

ABSOLUTELY NO TEXT. This overrides everything below. The panel is described as
holding a name, health values and four balances — every one of those is written
by the game engine at run time, in six languages, and changes while the game
runs. A painted number makes the panel unusable, because the real one lands on
top of it. Where a description mentions a name or a number, leave that area as
plain empty background.

  - no text, no letters, no numbers, no captions, no labels, no watermark
  - no icons inside any socket — the engine puts them there
  - no drop shadow onto the background, no ground plane, no scenery

INTERIOR
The body of the panel is OPAQUE: dark, even, slightly textured stone and glass
filling everything inside the ornate border. The game world lies behind it and
must not show through. Keep the surface calm — names, numbers and coloured bars
are drawn over it.

The only transparent parts are the sockets listed below. Each socket is a hole
running through the asset, because the engine draws underneath it: a portrait,
a coloured bar, a currency icon. Every socket has its own thin gold rim and a
bevelled inner wall, so it reads as cut into the metal.

STYLE
Dark polished obsidian and carved dark stone. Warm antique gold and bronze
trim, colour #c9a86a. Amethyst purple diamond-cut gems, colour #8b45d6, at the
corners. Thin double-line gold border with small notched corners. Soft rim
light from the upper left, gentle inner bevel, faint inner shadow where a
socket meets the surface. Realistic rendered metal and stone; not flat vector,
not cartoon outline, not neon.

BACKGROUND AND ALPHA
Transparent background around the panel and inside every socket. If the tool
cannot output transparency, use pure magenta #FF00FF for both — never black,
because the panel itself is almost black.

LAYOUT OF THE SHEET
The two assets side by side with a wide empty margin around each. Nothing
touching. No grid lines, no labels. Highest resolution available.

FOR EACH ASSET, ALSO WRITE A SHORT DESCRIPTION
Number, what it is, aspect ratio, and a list of its sockets with their
approximate positions as fractions of the panel — I place them by those.

G1 — WIDE STAT AND CURRENCY PANEL, aspect about 3.6 : 1
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
One horizontal panel. Opaque body, ornate gold border, an amethyst diamond at

  • FAR LEFT — one large circular socket for the character portrait, about

  • UPPER MIDDLE AND RIGHT — a plain empty band running from just right of

  • BELOW IT — two long horizontal grooves stacked one above the other, both

  • ACROSS THE BOTTOM — four small circular sockets in ONE row, evenly spaced
    across the FULL width of the panel including under the portrait, all the
