ЗАЧЕМ ЭТА ПАРТИЯ
  Панели первой партии — A1 (статы), A2 (карта), B3 (валюта) — сделаны со
  СКВОЗНОЙ серединой. Это правильно для рамки, под которую кладут карту или
  портрет, но у шапки сквозь них видно мир, и читается это плохо: цифры
  баланса лежат прямо на подземелье. Нужны те же панели, но с собственным
  тёмным фоном внутри.
  Плюс рамка врага в прицеле — её должно быть видно как ВРАЖДЕБНУЮ, — и
  оправа под бафы, которых сейчас нет вовсе и они лежат на экране голыми
  квадратами.

  1. ref/hud.png              — образец стиля

  3. скриншот с врагом в цели — рамка «Разъярённый Орк» с полосой HP
  4. скриншот с бафами        — ряд квадратов, которые сейчас лежат поверх UI

===PROMPT===

Use the attached game HUD mockup (the ornate dark-fantasy one) as the single
style reference. The other screenshots show the game as it is now — they are
there to show what is being replaced and in what proportion. Match the style of
the first image.

Produce a sheet of separate UI assets.

ABSOLUTELY NO TEXT. This overrides every description below. Several items are
described as panels that will show a name, a number or a status line — all of
that is written by the game engine at run time, in six languages, and it
changes while the game runs. A painted caption makes the panel unusable,
because the real one lands on top of it. Where a description mentions text,
leave that area as plain empty background.

  - no text, no letters, no numbers, no captions, no labels, no watermark
  - no icons inside a panel unless the description asks for one
  - no drop shadow onto the background, no ground plane, no scenery

WHAT IS DIFFERENT ABOUT THIS BATCH
Items F1 to F4 must have an OPAQUE interior: a dark, even, slightly textured
stone-and-glass surface filling the whole area inside the ornate border. Not
transparent, not a hole — the game world lies behind these panels and must not
show through. Keep the interior calm and free of detail in the middle, because
names, numbers and bars are drawn over it.

Items F5 and F6 are the opposite and are marked HOLLOW.

STYLE
Dark polished obsidian and carved dark stone. Warm antique gold and bronze
trim, colour #c9a86a. Amethyst purple diamond-cut gems, colour #8b45d6, at
corners and cardinal points. Thin double-line gold borders with small notched
corners. Soft rim light from the upper left, gentle inner bevel, faint inner
shadow where the interior meets the border. Realistic rendered metal, stone and
glass; not flat vector, not cartoon outline, not neon.

BACKGROUND AND ALPHA
Transparent background around each item. If the tool cannot output
transparency, use pure magenta #FF00FF for the background and for the hollow
sockets of F5 and F6 — never black, because the assets themselves are almost
black.

LAYOUT
One item per cell on a regular grid, each centred, upright, orthographic front
view, with a wide empty margin around it. No grid lines, no labels. Highest
resolution available.

FOR EACH ITEM, ALSO WRITE A SHORT DESCRIPTION
Output a plain-text list next to the image: number, what it is, aspect ratio,
and whether the interior is opaque or hollow.
